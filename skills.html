Thoughtful websites, built around your idea

What I bring to a project

I build front-end websites with custom HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, with a focus on making information clear and the experience easy to explore. Whether you need a site to introduce your business, explain a product, or share a subject you care about, I can shape the layout, content, and interactive details around the people who will use it.

  • Responsive front-end design: layouts that adapt to desktop and mobile screens
  • Clear content structure: organize information so visitors can find what matters
  • Custom visual design: HTML and CSS tailored to your subject, audience, and style
  • Audience-first thinking: present a topic in a way that works for both newcomers and people who already know it

Proof in the projects

My projects show both initiative and the ability to build for a specific request.

Self-directed projects

I chose to create the Fire Emblem Awakening site and the Persona site on my own. Taking these ideas from concept to finished websites helped me practice planning content, building connected pages, and giving each site a clear identity. The Fire Emblem site goes beyond static information with interactive character features, while the Persona site brings together several games, character pages, and related content. Together, they show that I can stick with a project, explore a subject in depth, and make a large amount of information easier to navigate.

Building to someone else's request

Osciloom was requested by a family member. Creating its product page gave me the opportunity to present a complex digital audio workstation in a more approachable way: introducing the product, organizing its features, and shaping a responsive page around its identity. The other two projects began with my own ideas; Osciloom shows that I can also take someone else's request and turn it into a focused web experience.
